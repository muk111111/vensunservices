About Vensun

Simplifying Business Operations Through One Integrated Partner

Vensun Global Services provides integrated B2B solutions across supply, packaging, manpower, logistics, warehousing and fulfilment.

Our goal is simple: help businesses reduce operational complexity by bringing multiple essential services under one reliable partner.

  • Fewer vendors to manage and coordinate
  • Consistent supply of essential business materials
  • Infrastructure and manpower that scale with demand

Our Services

01

Trading & B2B Supply

02

Packaging Materials

Bubble rolls, corrugated boxes, tapes, labels, stationery and other packaging and operational consumables.

03

Manpower Services

Reliable manpower for warehouses, offices and business operations, including packing, sorting, inventory handling, loading/unloading and administrative support.

04

Transportation & Logistics

Movement of goods between vendors, warehouses and customers through owned or third-party transportation networks.

05

Warehousing

Fulfilment Services

Supply Chain Services
